<h1 align="center">app</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Syner OS, at syner.app.</strong>
</p>

The control panel where your organization sees what its agents did with the
cloud and steps in when something changes. It answers five questions about your
apps: Is it working? What changed? What is it costing? Who has access? Is it
fulfilling its purpose?

Syner OS is itself an app on Syner, and the base from which other apps
are created. Syner Bot operates it the same way you do.
